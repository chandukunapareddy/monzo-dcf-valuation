# Monzo Bank – DCF Valuation

A three-statement financial model and discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation of Monzo Bank, built from its published Companies House accounts (2023–2026).

## What the model does
- Builds a linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement
- Calculates WACC using CAPM
- Projects free cash flows and values the business with a DCF
- Tests how the valuation changes under different assumptions
**[View the interactive dashboard](https://chandukunapareddy.github.io/monzo-dcf-valuation/monzo-dcf-dashboard.html)**
## Key results
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| WACC | 9.60% (risk-free rate 4.3%, beta 1.5, market risk premium 3.7%) |
| Enterprise value | £19.05 billion |
| Sensitivity range | £10.5–20.6 billion (across WACC and terminal growth) |

## What I found
My model valued Monzo above the level implied by the market. When I looked into the gap, it was mostly explained by a 25–30% regulatory risk premium the market seems to apply to UK challenger banks. In other words, investors are discounting for regulatory uncertainty, not doubting the growth.
